Creating your account is the first step to sending WhatsApp messages from ChatFlows.io. Your account gives you your own login and access to the platform. It only takes a few minutes to get started.

<Info>
  Choosing the **free trial** costs nothing and needs no payment for its first 14 days — after
  that, you'll need to pick a paid plan to keep going. See
  [Trial vs. paid plans](/getting-started/free-vs-paid).
</Info>

## Step 1: Pick a plan

<Figure
  src="/images/step1-pricing-plans.png"
  alt="The pricing page showing Free and Professional plan cards"
  caption="Start on the pricing page and choose how you want to begin."
/>

Open the sign-up form and choose how you'd like to start:

- **Free trial** — no payment, no card, 14 days to try everything out.
- **Professional** — pick this and you'll be asked to pay once you first log in.

<Tip>
  Not sure? Start on the **free trial** — nothing is locked away, and moving to a paid plan later
  keeps everything you've built. [Trial vs. paid plans](/getting-started/free-vs-paid) shows what
  each one includes.
</Tip>

## Step 2: Fill in the sign-up form

The sign-up form is a three-part wizard: **Personal Information**, **Company Information**, and
**Security**.

<Figure
  src="/images/step2-account-details.png"
  alt="Step 1 of the Create your workspace wizard, showing Sign up with Google and Facebook buttons above an Account details form with Full name, Email address, Password, Confirm password, WhatsApp number and Currency fields, and a Continue button"
  caption="An earlier version of this form — some fields shown here (WhatsApp number, Currency) have since been removed. Follow the field list below for what's actually on the form today."
/>

### Personal Information

- **Full name** and **Email address** — use an email you can open now; we may send a link to it.
- **Phone** — pick your country, then enter the number.
- **Country**.
- **Plan** — choose from the plans available to you. If you followed a link for a specific plan,
  it's already selected here.

<Tip>
  In a hurry? Look for **Sign up with Google** or a similar option above the form — one tap fills
  in your name and email and skips the password fields.
</Tip>

### Company Information

<Figure
  src="/images/step2-workspace-details.png"
  alt="Step 2 of the Create your workspace wizard, showing a Workspace name field, a Subdomain field with a live preview confirming the address is available, both policy agreement checkboxes ticked, and a Create workspace button"
  caption="An earlier version of this form — the field labelled 'Subdomain' here is now called 'Tenant Name' and no longer sets a web address. Follow the field list below for what's actually on the form today."
/>

- **Workspace** — your business name, e.g. "Acme Corp".
- **Tenant Name** — a unique handle for your account, checked live for availability. Lowercase
  letters and numbers only.
- **Address** — optional.

### Security

- **Password** — at least 8 characters, with an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number
  and a symbol. You'll type it twice, in **Password** and **Confirm password**.
- Tick to agree to the **Terms of Service** and **Privacy Policy**.
  You can't continue until this is ticked.

Click **Create workspace**.

<Tip>
  Already have a workspace? Use the **"Already have a workspace? Log in"** link at the bottom of
  the form instead.
</Tip>

## Step 3: Log in to finish setting up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your email, if asked">
    Some workspaces ask you to confirm your email before you can log in. If yours does, open the
    email we sent and click **Verify Email Address**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in">
    Use the email and password you just set. See [Log in](/getting-started/log-in) if you need a
    hand.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Signed up with Google or a similar option? You're taken straight into your workspace — no
  separate login needed the first time.
</Note>

### If you chose the free trial

You land straight in your workspace, ready to go.

### If you chose a paid plan

The first time you log in, you'll see a short activation wizard:

<Figure
  src="/images/paid-plan.png"
  alt="The Activate Your Workspace checkout screen, with a four-step sidebar for Your Selected Plan, Billing Details, Payment Method and Complete Payment, a plan summary card showing the plan cost, tax and monthly total, a Change my mind button, and a Continue to Billing Details button"
  caption="An earlier version of this wizard — the step names shown here have since been simplified to Plan & Billing Details, Payment Method, and Complete Payment. Follow the steps below for the current flow."
/>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Plan & Billing Details">
    Confirm your plan, then fill in your company name, billing email, tax details and address —
    these appear on your invoices and can be updated later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Payment Method">
    Choose how you'd like to pay, from the options available to you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Payment">
    Pay online and your workspace opens as soon as payment succeeds. Or choose **bank transfer**,
    submit your payment proof, and you'll wait on a short approval screen while your provider
    confirms it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  For how the plans differ and how billing works, see
  [Plans & subscription](/billing/plans-and-subscription).
</Info>
