There are two ways in. Open your dashboard from your Shopify admin, or sign in with your email and password.

## Sign in from Shopify

In your Shopify admin, open ChatFlows.io and click **Open dashboard**. You're signed in to your workspace as the owner. You don't need a password.

If you've turned on two-factor security, you still enter your code.

## Set your password

You don't need a password to use ChatFlows.io from Shopify. A password lets you sign in from anywhere, without going through Shopify first.

When your workspace was created, you got a **Your workspace is ready** email. It has a link to set your password. The link works for 7 days.

Until you set a password, your dashboard shows a banner: **Set a password to sign in without Shopify.** Click **Set a password** in the banner to choose one. You can also close the banner. It's up to you.

<Tip>
  Has the link in the email run out? Use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page to get a new one.
</Tip>

## Signing in with your password

On the **Sign in** page, enter your **Email address** and **Password**, then click **Log in**.
The **Forgot password?** link sits on the same row as **Remember me** — see below if you need it.

<Tip>
  You can also sign in with **Google** or **Facebook**, if your provider has turned these on —
  look for the buttons below the form. This works as long as that Google or Facebook account
  uses the same email as your ChatFlows.io account.
</Tip>

<Note>
  For security, sign-in is rate-limited: after several failed tries for the same email in a
  minute, you'll be asked to wait a short while before trying again. It clears on its own.
</Note>

<Note>
  If you see an **Account Suspended** screen instead of your workspace, your provider has paused
  your account — contact them to sort it out. You can't resolve this from your side.
</Note>

## Forgot your password

No problem — you can reset it yourself.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click 'Forgot your password?'">
    It's on the same row as Remember me, on the sign-in page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email">
    We'll email a reset link to that address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the link and set a new one">
    The link opens a **"Choose a new password"** page. Your new password needs at least 8
    characters, with an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number and a symbol.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If the email doesn't arrive, check your spam or junk folder. Reset links expire after a while,
  so if yours has, just request a new one.
</Note>

## Add two-factor security (2FA)

Want to make your account more secure? After you log in, go to your **Profile** and turn on
two-factor security. It asks for a short code from your phone on top of your password, so your
account stays safe even if someone learns the password.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn it on">
    Open the **Two-Factor Authentication** card in your profile and click **Enable**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan the code">
    Scan the QR code with an authenticator app — Google Authenticator, Authy, 1Password, or any
    similar one — then enter the 6-digit code it shows to confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your recovery codes">
    You'll get a set of one-time **recovery codes**. Keep them somewhere safe — any one of them
    logs you in if you ever lose your phone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After this, every login asks for the **6-digit code** from your app once you've entered your
password. Lost your phone? Choose **login using a recovery code** and use one of the codes you
saved.
