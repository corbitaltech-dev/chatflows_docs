Already using another WhatsApp app or provider? You don't start from zero.
You can keep your number, bring your approved templates, and bring your customers.

## Before you start

Find out how your number is connected today. It is one of these:

- **On the WhatsApp Business app** on your phone.
- **On another provider's WhatsApp API.**

## Move across in 5 steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install ChatFlows from Shopify" icon="shopping-bag">
    Install the app and pick a plan. See [Install from Shopify](/getting-started/install-from-shopify).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect your number" icon="message-circle">
    Go to **WhatsApp Setup** and click **Connect Channel**.

    - **Number on the WhatsApp Business app?** Connect it alongside the app. You keep using the app on your phone, and new messages show up in both places.
    - **Number on another provider's API?** Ask your old provider to release the number first. Then connect it in ChatFlows with Meta's pop-up window.

    Details: [Connect WhatsApp](/core-setup/channels/whatsapp)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Bring your templates" icon="file-text">
    If your number stays on the same WhatsApp Business account, go to **Message Templates → WhatsApp** and click **Sync Templates**.
    Your approved templates come in straight away, with no new review.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Bring your customers" icon="users">
    Go to **Settings → Shopify** and click **Sync contacts** to bring in your Shopify customers.
    Have a list from your old app? Export it as a spreadsheet and [import it](/contacts/import-export).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set up your automations again" icon="workflow">
    Automations don't move between apps. Build them again in **Automations**.
    Start with [cart reminders](/shopify/cart-recovery) and [order updates](/shopify/order-updates). Or describe what you want and let [AI build it](/automation/generate-with-ai).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Turn off your old app's cart reminders and order updates once ChatFlows is set up.
  Otherwise your customers may get the same message twice.
</Warning>

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will I lose my chat history?">
    Chats stay in your old app. If your number is on the WhatsApp Business app, recent chats come across when you connect alongside it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do my templates need to be approved again?">
    Not if your number stays on the same WhatsApp Business account. **Sync Templates** brings in the ones Meta already approved.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will my messaging limit reset?">
    Your messaging limit belongs to your number and your Meta business, not to the app you use. It stays the same when you switch.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
