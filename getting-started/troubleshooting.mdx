Start here when something doesn't work as you expect.
For any message, the first place to look is **Setup → WhatsApp Message Log**. It lists every message sent and shows why a message failed.
See [WhatsApp Message Log](/core-setup/whatsapp-activity-log).

## A message didn't send

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The customer hasn't written to you in 24 hours">
    Outside the 24 hours, you can only send an approved **template**. A normal text message won't go through.
    Start your automation or campaign with a template. See [the 24-hour rule](/getting-started/concepts).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The template isn't approved yet">
    A template can only be sent when it shows **Approved**. Check its status in **Message Templates → WhatsApp**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The customer opted out">
    A customer who replied **STOP** no longer gets marketing messages. Order updates and other Utility messages still go out.
    See [Opt-out](/settings/opt-out).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The customer has no phone number">
    WhatsApp needs a phone number. Check that your Shopify checkout asks for one.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Meta stopped the message">
    Meta can stop messages, for example when there's no payment method on your WhatsApp Business account, or you reached your messaging limit.
    The reason shows in the **WhatsApp Message Log**. See [WhatsApp costs](/billing/whatsapp-costs).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## A cart reminder wasn't sent

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The shopper ordered before the wait was over">
    That's expected. ChatFlows.io checks the checkout again before it sends. A shopper who ordered gets nothing.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The wait isn't over yet">
    Each cart automation waits for its **Send after (minutes)** time. Check what you set. See [Recover abandoned carts](/shopify/cart-recovery).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The automation is off">
    Go to **Automations** and check that the automation is switched on.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The shopper didn't give a phone number">
    Shopify only shares a phone number if your checkout asks for one.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## An order update didn't run

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Out for delivery or Delivered never runs">
    These two events need delivery tracking. Your shipping carrier or shipping app must send tracking updates to Shopify.
    If you mark orders as fulfilled by hand, these events never run.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Delivery updates are off">
    Your store was connected before ChatFlows.io asked for delivery access.
    Go to **Settings → Shopify** and click **Reconnect**, then approve the access in Shopify.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Nothing runs for any order">
    Go to **Settings → Shopify** and check that your store shows **Connected**.
    If it shows **Needs attention**, read the reason and click **Reconnect** or **Retry setup**.
    See [Your store and the Shopify app](/shopify/shopify-admin-app).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## A template was rejected

Meta reviews every template. If it's rejected, you get a notification. Common reasons:

- The category is wrong. For example, an offer inside a **Utility** template.
- The template starts or ends with a variable.
- The example values are missing or unclear.

Fix the wording and submit it again. See [Template examples](/messaging/template-examples) for wording that works.
