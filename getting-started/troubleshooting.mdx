Start here when something doesn't work as you expect.
For any message, the first place to look is **Setup → WhatsApp Message Log**. It lists every message sent and shows why a message failed.
See [WhatsApp Message Log](/core-setup/whatsapp-activity-log).

Can't connect your WhatsApp number at all? Go through the [checklist before you connect](/core-setup/channels/whatsapp#before-you-connect) and its common problems.

## A message didn't send

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The customer hasn't written to you in 24 hours">
    Outside the 24 hours, you can only send an approved **template**. A normal text message won't go through.
    Start your automation or campaign with a template. See [the two WhatsApp rules](/getting-started/concepts#two-whatsapp-rules-that-shape-everything).

    This is a WhatsApp rule, not a ChatFlows one. Meta explains it under [pricing](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The template isn't approved yet">
    A template can only be sent when it shows **Approved**. Check its status in **Message Templates → WhatsApp**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The customer opted out">
    A customer who replied **STOP** no longer gets marketing messages. Order updates and other Utility messages still go out.
    See [Opt-out](/settings/opt-out).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The customer has no phone number">
    WhatsApp can't reach anyone without a phone number. Make your Shopify checkout ask for one:

    1. In your Shopify admin, go to **Settings → Checkout**.
    2. Under **Customer information**, set **Shipping address phone number** to **Required**.
    3. Click **Save**.

    This only works when checkout has a shipping step. It doesn't change customers who have already ordered. Shopify explains it under [checkout form options](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/checkout-settings/checkout-form-options).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Meta stopped the message">
    Meta can stop messages, for example when there's no payment method on your WhatsApp Business account, or you reached your messaging limit.
    The reason shows in the **WhatsApp Message Log**. See [WhatsApp costs](/billing/whatsapp-costs).
    Meta explains daily limits under [messaging limits](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/messaging-limits).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## A cart reminder wasn't sent

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The shopper ordered before the wait was over">
    That's expected. ChatFlows checks the checkout again before it sends. A shopper who ordered gets nothing.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The wait isn't over yet">
    Each cart automation waits for its **Send after (minutes)** time. Check what you set. See [Recover abandoned carts](/shopify/cart-recovery).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The automation is off">
    Go to **Automations** and check that the automation is switched on.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The shopper didn't give a phone number">
    Shopify only shares a phone number if your checkout asks for one. To turn it on, see **The customer has no phone number** under **A message didn't send** above.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## An order update didn't run

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Out for delivery or Delivered never runs">
    These two events need delivery tracking. Your shipping carrier or shipping app must send tracking updates to Shopify.
    If you mark orders as fulfilled by hand, these events never run.

    **To check:** open the order in your Shopify admin. If it has no tracking number, or the tracking shows no delivery updates, Shopify has nothing to tell ChatFlows. Add tracking when you fulfill the order, or ask your shipping app whether it sends tracking updates to Shopify.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Delivery updates are off">
    Your store was connected before ChatFlows asked for delivery access.
    Go to **Settings → Shopify** and click **Reconnect**, then approve the access in Shopify.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Nothing runs for any order">
    Go to **Settings → Shopify** and check that your store shows **Connected**.
    If it shows **Needs attention**, read the reason and click **Reconnect** or **Retry setup**.
    See [Your store and the Shopify app](/shopify/shopify-admin-app).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## A template was rejected

Meta reviews every template. If it's rejected, you get a notification, and the template shows **Rejected** in **Message Templates → WhatsApp**.

Common reasons:

- The category is wrong. For example, an offer inside a **Utility** template.
- The template starts or ends with a variable.
- The example values are missing or unclear.

Edit the template and submit it again, or delete it and start over. See [Template examples](/messaging/template-examples) for wording that works.

<Note>
  Meta decides what's approved, not ChatFlows. Meta explains categories and reviews under [templates](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/templates/overview).
</Note>
