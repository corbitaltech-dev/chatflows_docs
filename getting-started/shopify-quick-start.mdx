ChatFlows.io connects your Shopify store to your own WhatsApp Business number. Cart reminders, order updates and campaigns go out on WhatsApp, and your team answers every reply from one inbox.

This page is for stores that install ChatFlows.io from the **Shopify App Store**. Your plan is billed on your Shopify invoice.

## What you get

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Carts brought back" icon="shopping-cart">
    A shopper who leaves checkout gets a friendly WhatsApp reminder with a link straight back to their cart.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Fewer &quot;Where's my order?&quot; messages" icon="truck">
    Customers hear when their order is placed, shipped, out for delivery and delivered.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Campaigns customers can reply to" icon="megaphone">
    Send a new collection or an offer to everyone, or to a group built from your Shopify tags.
  </Card>

  <Card title="One inbox for the team" icon="messages-square">
    Every reply lands in a shared inbox, so anyone on your team can pick it up.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Before you start

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A phone number for WhatsApp" icon="smartphone">
    You need a phone number that can be used on WhatsApp's business platform. Already using the **WhatsApp Business app** on that number? You can keep using it alongside ChatFlows.io. Details: [WhatsApp channel](/core-setup/channels/whatsapp)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A Meta (Facebook) business account" icon="building-2">
    WhatsApp is run by Meta, so you connect your number by signing in with Meta. If you don't have a business account yet, Meta's own pop-up window lets you create one while you connect.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Customers' phone numbers in Shopify" icon="contact">
    WhatsApp can only reach a customer who gave a phone number at checkout or on their customer profile. Check that your Shopify checkout asks for a phone number.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## The steps

<Steps>
<Step title="Install ChatFlows.io from the Shopify App Store">
  Open [ChatFlows.io on the Shopify App Store](https://apps.shopify.com/chatflows) and click **Install**. Shopify shows what ChatFlows.io can read in your store. ChatFlows.io only reads orders, checkouts and customers; it never changes them.

  Details: [Install from Shopify](/getting-started/install-from-shopify)
</Step>

<Step title="Choose a plan">
  Pick a plan inside your Shopify admin and approve it on Shopify's billing screen. Your workspace is created and your store shows as **Connected**.

  Already have a ChatFlows.io workspace? Choose **Sign in to link this store** instead of creating a new one.
</Step>

<Step title="Open your dashboard">
  On the ChatFlows.io page in your Shopify admin, click **Open dashboard**. It opens ChatFlows.io already signed in.
</Step>

<Step title="Connect your WhatsApp number">
  Go to **WhatsApp Setup** and click **Connect Channel**. Sign in with Meta and pick your number. Meta's own pop-up window walks you through it.

  Details: [WhatsApp channel](/core-setup/channels/whatsapp)
</Step>

<Step title="Get your first message approved">
  The first message to a customer must be a **template**: a reusable message format with blanks for each customer's name, order number and so on. Meta checks each one before it can be sent.

  Details: [WhatsApp templates](/messaging/whatsapp-templates)
</Step>

<Step title="Switch on cart reminders and order updates">
  Create an automation that starts from a Shopify event, such as **Cart abandoned** or **Order shipped**, and sends your approved template. Turn it on.

  Details: [Shopify triggers](/automation/flow-builder#shopify)
</Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  **Done.** Install from Shopify → choose a plan → connect your number → get a template approved → switch on an automation.
</Check>

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How is my plan billed?">
    On your normal Shopify invoice. You choose, change or cancel your plan inside your Shopify admin. Uninstalling the app cancels it. See [Plan and billing](/billing/plan-and-billing).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does WhatsApp charge me separately?">
    Yes. Meta charges for template messages, and the rate depends on the kind of template and the customer's country. Meta bills you directly, and ChatFlows.io adds nothing on top. Meta lists its rates on its [pricing page](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I send a second cart reminder?">
    Yes. Each cart reminder is its own automation with its own **Send after (minutes)** delay, up to 24 hours after checkout starts. Before any reminder goes out, ChatFlows.io checks the checkout again, so a shopper who has already ordered gets nothing more.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why can't I just type a message to a customer?">
    WhatsApp only lets a business start a conversation with a template Meta has approved. Once the customer writes back, you can write freely for 24 hours. This is a WhatsApp rule, not a ChatFlows.io one. Meta explains it in its [template overview](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/templates/overview).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will I lose my WhatsApp number or my old chats?">
    No. If your number is on the WhatsApp Business app, ChatFlows.io connects alongside it. You keep the app on your phone, and new messages show up in both places.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does ChatFlows.io confirm COD orders before they ship?">
    Not as a built-in step. For cash-on-delivery orders, ChatFlows.io sends the same order and shipping updates as for any order, plus a message once the COD payment is marked as received in Shopify.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Who can I message?">
    Customers who agreed to hear from your business on WhatsApp. That is WhatsApp's rule; Meta sets it out in the [WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy](https://whatsappbusiness.com/policy/).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
