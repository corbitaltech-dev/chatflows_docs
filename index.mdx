Win back abandoned carts, stop answering "Where's my order?" and send campaigns your customers can reply to.
ChatFlows connects your Shopify store to your own WhatsApp Business number, and your team answers every reply from one inbox.

## How ChatFlows works with your store

You install ChatFlows from the Shopify App Store, just like any other app. The install creates your ChatFlows workspace and links it to your store, so you don't need to sign up anywhere else.

While you install, Shopify asks you to let ChatFlows see your orders, checkouts and delivery updates. This is how ChatFlows knows what happens in your store, and it lets ChatFlows do three things for you:

- **Send messages at the right moment.** When a customer leaves the checkout without buying, or when an order is placed, shipped or delivered, ChatFlows sends the WhatsApp message you set up for it.
- **Build your customer list.** Everyone who checks out or places an order is added to **Customers** automatically. You can also bring in the customers already in your store with one click.
- **Show your results in Shopify.** The ChatFlows app in your Shopify admin shows your orders, sales and the carts you won back.

Learn more in [Your store and the Shopify app](/shopify/shopify-admin-app).

## Before you start

Have these five things ready, so nothing stops you halfway through the setup.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Permission to add apps to your Shopify store" icon="shopping-bag">
    You install ChatFlows from the Shopify App Store. If you're the store owner, you already have this.
    If someone else runs the store, ask them to give you permission to add apps.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A phone number for WhatsApp" icon="smartphone">
    The number your customers will see your messages come from. It can be:

    - **A number that isn't on WhatsApp yet.**
    - **A number on the WhatsApp Business app.** You keep using the app on your phone, and new messages show up in both places.

    Is the number on the personal WhatsApp app? Delete that WhatsApp account on the phone first, or use another number.
    Is it with another WhatsApp provider? Ask them to release it first.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A Facebook account" icon="building-2">
    WhatsApp belongs to Meta, so you connect your number by signing in with Facebook.
    No Meta Business account yet? Meta's own pop-up window lets you create one while you connect.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A payment method with Meta" icon="credit-card">
    Meta charges for template messages, and for your replies after the first 1,000 each month. Meta bills you directly, so add a payment method in Meta's WhatsApp Manager.
    Without it, your templates won't send. See [WhatsApp costs](/billing/whatsapp-costs).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Customers' phone numbers in Shopify" icon="contact">
    WhatsApp can only reach customers who gave a phone number.
    Check that your Shopify checkout asks for one. See [how to turn it on](/shopify/cart-recovery#collect-phone-numbers-at-checkout).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Set up in 6 steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install ChatFlows from Shopify" icon="shopping-bag">
    Install [ChatFlows: WhatsApp Automation](https://apps.shopify.com/chatflows) from the Shopify App Store and approve your plan. Then click **Open dashboard**.

    Details: [Install from Shopify](/getting-started/install-from-shopify)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect your WhatsApp number" icon="message-circle">
    Go to **WhatsApp Setup** and click **Connect Channel**. Choose **WhatsApp**, then sign in with Meta and pick your number in Meta's pop-up window.

    When the pop-up closes, your number shows as **Active**.

    Not sure you're ready? Check the [5-point checklist](/core-setup/channels/whatsapp#before-you-connect) first.

    Details: [Connect WhatsApp](/core-setup/channels/whatsapp)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get your first message approved" icon="file-text">
    The first message to a customer must be a **template**: a reusable message with blanks for things like the customer's name or order number. Meta checks each template before you can send it.

    Go to **Message Templates → WhatsApp → Add Template**, write your message and submit it.
    Not sure what to write? Start from one of the [ready-made template examples](/messaging/template-examples).

    Your template shows **Pending** while Meta reviews it. Wait until it shows **Approved** before you use it.

    Already have templates in your Meta account, or moving from another WhatsApp app? Click **Sync Templates** to bring them in.

    Details: [WhatsApp templates](/messaging/whatsapp-templates)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send yourself a test" icon="smartphone">
    Go to **WhatsApp Setup** and click **Test** on your WhatsApp channel. Pick your approved template, enter your own phone number and send it.

    When it arrives on your phone, you know everything works.

    Details: [Test message](/core-setup/channels/whatsapp#test-message)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Switch on cart reminders and order updates" icon="shopping-cart">
    Go to **Automations** and create an automation. Click the **Start Trigger**, choose **Shopify**, and pick an event such as **Cart abandoned** or **Order shipped**.
    Add your approved template and turn the automation on.

    From now on, ChatFlows sends the message each time that event happens in your store.

    Details: [Recover abandoned carts](/shopify/cart-recovery) · [Send order updates](/shopify/order-updates)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Bring in your customers and send a campaign" icon="send">
    Go to **Settings → Shopify** and click **Sync contacts** to bring in your Shopify customers.
    Then go to **Campaigns**, create a campaign and choose **Template** as the campaign type. Send it to everyone or to one group.

    Details: [Your store and the Shopify app](/shopify/shopify-admin-app#bring-in-your-shopify-customers) · [Campaigns](/messaging/campaigns)
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  **You're live.** Install from Shopify → connect your number → get a template approved → send yourself a test → switch on an automation → send a campaign.
</Check>

## When customers reply

Replies land in the **Inbox**. Anyone on your team can pick up a chat, assign it, or leave a private note.

Once a customer writes to you, you can reply in your own words for 24 hours. After that, you need an approved template again.
This is a WhatsApp rule, not a ChatFlows one. [The short version of the rules →](/getting-started/concepts)

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will I lose my WhatsApp number or my old chats?">
    No. If your number is on the WhatsApp Business app, ChatFlows connects alongside it.
    You keep the app on your phone, and new messages show up in both places.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I use another WhatsApp app. Can I switch?">
    Yes. Keep your number, sync your approved templates, and bring in your customers.
    See [Switch from another app](/getting-started/switch-from-another-app).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How is my plan billed?">
    On your normal Shopify invoice. You choose, change or cancel your plan in your Shopify admin. Uninstalling the app cancels it.
    See [Plan and billing](/billing/plan-and-billing).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does WhatsApp charge me separately?">
    Yes. Meta charges for template messages, and for your replies after the first 1,000 each month. The rate depends on the kind of message and the customer's country.
    Meta bills you directly, and ChatFlows adds nothing on top. See [WhatsApp costs](/billing/whatsapp-costs) for an example monthly bill.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why can't I just type a message to a new customer?">
    WhatsApp only lets a business start a conversation with a template Meta has approved.
    Once the customer replies, you can write in your own words for 24 hours. Meta explains it in its [template overview](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/templates/overview).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Who can I message?">
    Customers who agreed to hear from your business on WhatsApp. This is WhatsApp's rule.
    Meta sets it out in the [WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy](https://whatsappbusiness.com/policy/).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I send a second cart reminder?">
    Yes. Make a second cart automation with a longer **Send after (minutes)** delay.
    Before any reminder goes out, ChatFlows checks the checkout again, so a customer who has already ordered gets nothing more.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does ChatFlows confirm COD orders before they ship?">
    Yes. ChatFlows asks each cash-on-delivery buyer to confirm or cancel on WhatsApp, and tags the order **COD-CONFIRMED** or **COD-CANCELLED** in Shopify so your team packs only confirmed orders. See [COD orders](/shopify/cod-orders).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
