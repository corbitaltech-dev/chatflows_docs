ChatFlows.io connects your Shopify store to your WhatsApp Business number.
Shoppers who leave at checkout get a reminder. Buyers hear when their order ships and arrives.
Your team answers every reply from one shared inbox.

## What you get

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Carts brought back" icon="shopping-cart">
    A shopper who leaves checkout gets a WhatsApp reminder with a link straight back to their cart.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Fewer &quot;Where's my order?&quot; messages" icon="package">
    Buyers hear when their order is placed, shipped, out for delivery and delivered.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Campaigns customers can reply to" icon="megaphone">

## Before you start

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A Shopify store" icon="shopping-bag">
    You install ChatFlows.io from the Shopify App Store, so you need permission to add apps to your store.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A phone number for WhatsApp" icon="smartphone">
    Use a number that isn't on WhatsApp yet, or one that already uses the **WhatsApp Business app**.

    Already on the WhatsApp Business app? You can keep using it on your phone. ChatFlows.io connects alongside it, and new messages show up in both places.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A Meta (Facebook) business account" icon="building-2">
    WhatsApp is run by Meta, so you connect your number by signing in with Meta.
    No business account yet? Meta's own pop-up window lets you create one while you connect.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Customers' phone numbers in Shopify" icon="contact">
    WhatsApp can only reach a customer who gave a phone number.
    Check that your Shopify checkout asks for a phone number.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Set up in 5 steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install ChatFlows.io from Shopify" icon="shopping-bag">
    Open [ChatFlows: WhatsApp Automation](https://apps.shopify.com/chatflows) in the Shopify App Store and click **Install**.
    Pick a plan and approve it in Shopify. Your workspace is created and your store is connected.

    Then click **Open dashboard**. You are signed in straight away.

    Details: [Install from Shopify](/getting-started/install-from-shopify)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect your WhatsApp number" icon="message-circle">
    Go to **WhatsApp Setup** and click **Connect Channel**. Choose **WhatsApp**, then sign in with Meta and pick your number in Meta's pop-up window.

    When the pop-up closes, your number shows as **Active**.

    Details: [Connect WhatsApp](/core-setup/channels/whatsapp)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get your first message approved" icon="file-text">
    The first message to a customer must be a **template**: a reusable message with blanks for things like the customer's name or order number. Meta checks each template before you can send it.

    Go to **Message Templates → WhatsApp → Add Template**, write your message and submit it. Approval usually takes a few minutes.

    Moving from another WhatsApp app? Click **Sync Templates** to bring in the templates you already have.

    Details: [WhatsApp templates](/messaging/whatsapp-templates)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Switch on cart reminders and order updates" icon="shopping-cart">
    Go to **Automations** and create an automation. Click the **Start Trigger**, choose **Shopify**, and pick an event such as **Cart abandoned** or **Order shipped**.
    Add your approved template and turn the automation on.

    Details: [Recover abandoned carts](/shopify/cart-recovery) · [Send order updates](/shopify/order-updates)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Bring in your customers and send a campaign" icon="send">
    Go to **Settings → Shopify** and click **Sync contacts** to bring in your Shopify customers.
    Then go to **Campaigns → Add Campaign**, choose **Template Campaign**, and send your template to everyone or to one group.

    Details: [Your store and the Shopify app](/shopify/shopify-admin-app#bring-in-your-shopify-customers) · [Campaigns](/messaging/campaigns)
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  **Done.** Install from Shopify → connect your number → get a template approved → switch on an automation → send a campaign.
</Check>

## When customers reply

Replies land in the **Inbox**. Anyone on your team can pick up a chat, assign it, or leave a private note.

Once a customer writes to you, you can reply freely for 24 hours. After that, you need an approved template again.
This is a WhatsApp rule, not a ChatFlows.io one. [The short version of the rules →](/getting-started/concepts)

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How is my plan billed?">
    On your normal Shopify invoice. You choose, change or cancel your plan in your Shopify admin. Uninstalling the app cancels it.
    See [Plan and billing](/billing/plan-and-billing).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does WhatsApp charge me separately?">
    Yes. Meta charges for template messages. The rate depends on the kind of template and the customer's country.
    Meta bills you directly, and ChatFlows.io adds nothing on top. Meta lists its rates on its [pricing page](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I send a second cart reminder?">
    Yes. Make a second cart automation with a longer **Send after (minutes)** delay.
    Before any reminder goes out, ChatFlows.io checks the checkout again, so a shopper who has already ordered gets nothing more.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does ChatFlows.io confirm COD orders before they ship?">
    Not as a built-in step. For cash-on-delivery orders, ChatFlows.io sends the same order and shipping updates as for any order.
    The **COD order confirmed** event sends a message once the COD payment is marked as paid in Shopify.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will I lose my WhatsApp number or my old chats?">
    No. If your number is on the WhatsApp Business app, ChatFlows.io connects alongside it.
    You keep the app on your phone, and new messages show up in both places.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why can't I just type a message to a new customer?">
    WhatsApp only lets a business start a conversation with a template Meta has approved.
    Once the customer replies, you can write freely for 24 hours. Meta explains it in its [template overview](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/templates/overview).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Who can I message?">
    Customers who agreed to hear from your business on WhatsApp. This is WhatsApp's rule.
    Meta sets it out in the [WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy](https://whatsappbusiness.com/policy/).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
