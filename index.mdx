ChatFlows.io connects your Shopify store to WhatsApp. It reminds shoppers about the carts they left behind and keeps buyers posted on their orders. Your team answers every reply from one shared inbox.

  </Card>

  </Card>

  <Card title="One inbox for the team" icon="messages-square">

## Before you start

Three things to have ready:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A Shopify store" icon="shopping-bag">
    You install ChatFlows.io from the Shopify App Store, so you need to be able to add apps to your store.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A phone number for WhatsApp" icon="smartphone">
    It needs to be **either**:

    - Not currently registered on WhatsApp, **or**
    - Already being used with the **WhatsApp Business app**.

    <Tip>
      Already running your business on the WhatsApp Business app? You **don't** have to give it up. ChatFlows.io can connect alongside it — you keep using the app on your phone, your old chats come across, and new messages appear in both places.
    </Tip>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A Facebook account with a business profile on Meta" icon="facebook">
    WhatsApp is owned by Meta, so setting up a business number through **WhatsApp Cloud API** requires a Meta Business account.

    A **Meta Business account** is enough to get started. You may not need to complete business verification during the initial setup.

    However, completing Meta's business verification can increase your messaging limits, so it is recommended when you are ready to send more messages.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Set up in 6 steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install ChatFlows.io from Shopify" icon="shopping-bag">
    Add ChatFlows.io to your store from the Shopify App Store. Installing it connects your store and creates your workspace.

    To open your workspace, go to ChatFlows.io in your Shopify admin and click **Open dashboard**.

    Details: [Install from Shopify](/getting-started/install-from-shopify)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect your WhatsApp number" icon="message-circle">
    Nothing can be sent or received until a number is connected. Go to **WhatsApp Setup → Connect Channel**, choose **WhatsApp**, and use **Embedded Signup** — Meta's own pop-up window walks you through logging in and picking your number.

    When the pop-up closes, your number should show as **Active**. That's it.

    <Tip>
      You can connect more than one number, and a Facebook Messenger page too, and handle them all from the same inbox.
    </Tip>

    Details: [Connecting WhatsApp](/core-setup/channels/whatsapp)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get one message approved" icon="file-text">
    Before you can send the first message to someone, WhatsApp requires you to use an **approved message template**. This applies to every business on WhatsApp, not just ChatFlows.io.

    A **message template** is a reusable message that you can personalize with details like the customer's name or order number.

    - **Starting fresh?** Go to **Message Templates → WhatsApp → Add Template**, write your message, and submit it. Approval usually takes a few minutes.
    - **Already using another WhatsApp provider?** Click **Sync Templates** and every message you've already had approved is pulled in instantly — no waiting at all.

    <Warning>
      A template has to show **APPROVED** before you can send it. This is the step most likely to keep you waiting, so start it early — the review runs in the background while you carry on with the next step.
    </Warning>

    Details: [WhatsApp templates](/messaging/whatsapp-templates)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Recover your first abandoned cart" icon="shopping-cart">
    Create an automation that starts when a cart is abandoned. Choose how long to wait, then send your approved template with a link back to the cart. If the shopper buys before the wait is over, no message is sent.

    Details: [Recover abandoned carts](/shopify/cart-recovery)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add the people you want to message" icon="contact">
    Go to **Customers** and click **Add Contact**. One person is enough to test with — use your own number if you like. If you already have a customer list, click **Import Contacts** instead and upload a CSV or spreadsheet.

    Details: [Managing contacts](/contacts/managing-contacts) · [Importing a list](/contacts/import-export)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send your first message" icon="send">
    Two ways to send your first message:

    1. **To one person** — find them in your **Customers** list, open the row's action menu (the three dots on the right), and choose **Initiate chat**. Pick your approved template and send.
    2. **To everyone** — go to **Campaigns → Add Campaign** and choose **Template Campaign**, which sends your approved template to a whole group of customers.

    Then watch the delivered and read counts come in.

    <Note>
      You'll also see a **Message Campaign** option, which sends free-form messages in your own wording. It only reaches people who messaged you in the last 24 hours, so it can't be your *first* message to a new customer. Start with a Template Campaign.
    </Note>

    Details: [Campaigns](/messaging/campaigns)
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  **Done.** Install from Shopify → connect a number → get one message approved → recover carts → add customers → send. Everything else in ChatFlows.io builds on top of these steps.
</Check>

## When people reply

Replies land in the **Inbox** — your shared inbox. Anyone on your team can pick up a conversation, assign it to a colleague, leave a private note, or send a saved reply.

One thing worth knowing: once a customer messages you, you can write back **freely** for 24 hours — any wording you like. After 24 hours of silence from them, you can only reach out again using an approved template. That single rule explains most "why can't I send this?" moments. ## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will I lose my existing WhatsApp number or my old chats?">
    No. If your number is on the **WhatsApp Business app**, ChatFlows.io can connect alongside it — you keep the app on your phone, your past conversations are brought in, and new messages show up in both places.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why can't I just type whatever I want to a new customer?">
    WhatsApp only lets businesses open a conversation with wording it has approved in advance — that's what a template is. Once the customer replies, you can write freely for the next 24 hours. [More on the rules](/getting-started/concepts).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long does approval take?">
    Usually a few minutes. Occasionally longer. You'll get a notification the moment the status changes, and you can carry on setting everything else up while you wait.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I'm already with another WhatsApp provider. Can I move across?">
    Yes, and you don't start from zero. Connect the same number, then click **Sync Templates** on the **Message Templates** page to pull in every message you've already had approved. Your customers come across from a spreadsheet, or straight from your Shopify store.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I connect ChatFlows.io to my own software?">
    Yes, if you want to. There's a [developer API](/overview) for sending messages and managing contacts from your own systems. Nothing in this guide needs it — everything above is done from the screen.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
