Your WhatsApp number is what your customers see. Connect it once, and ChatFlows can send your cart reminders, order updates and campaigns from it, and every reply lands in your **Inbox**.

Meta's own pop-up window walks you through it. Behind your number sits a WhatsApp Business Account (Meta calls it a **WABA**), which you create or pick in that window.

## Before you connect

<Tip>
Make sure you have the following ready:

- A Facebook account.
- A **Meta Business account** — Business Verification is not mandatory to connect, but it is strongly recommended; without it your account stays on limited messaging tiers.
- One mobile number for WhatsApp that must be either:
  - **not connected to any WhatsApp account**, or
  - **already connected to the WhatsApp Business App**
- A **payment method** in Meta's WhatsApp Manager. Meta needs it to send your template messages. See [WhatsApp costs](/billing/whatsapp-costs).
- Your **WABA ID**, **Access Token**, **Meta App ID**, and **App Secret** (for manual connections only).
</Tip>

## Connecting WhatsApp

<Steps>
<Step title="Click Connect">
  Go to **WhatsApp Setup**, click **Connect Channel**, choose **WhatsApp**, then click **Continue**.

  <Figure
    src="/core-setup/channels/img/channels-connect.png"
    alt="WhatsApp Setup page showing a Connect Channel button and a Supported channels panel listing WhatsApp and Messenger, both marked Available"
    caption="Image shows the WhatsApp Setup page with the Connect Channel button."
  />
</Step>

<Step title="Pick a connection method">
  You'll see two tabs:

  <Figure
    src="/core-setup/channels/img/whatsapp-connect-methods.png"
    alt="Connect a WhatsApp Account sheet shown twice: the Embedded Signup tab with four setup steps, a Channel Name field and a Launch button, and the Manual tab with fields for channel name, Business Account ID, access token, Meta App ID, and App Secret"
    caption="Image shows the Connect a WhatsApp Account sheet with its Embedded Signup and Manual tabs."
  />

  - **Embedded Signup** — connect with Meta's secure sign-up window.
    - Enter a **Channel Name**, then click **Launch**.
    - Log in to your Facebook account.
    - Choose your Business Manager and WhatsApp number.
    - Grant the WhatsApp permissions.
    Your channel is created automatically when you finish.
  - **Manual** — use this for advanced setups with existing WhatsApp Business API credentials.
    - Enter the **WABA ID**.
    - Paste the **Access Token**.
    - Provide the **Meta App ID** and **App Secret**.
</Step>

<Step title="Confirm it's connected">
  Once connected, the channel appears in your list on the **WhatsApp Setup** page with an **Active** status, and a webhook subscription is set up automatically so incoming messages reach your workspace.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Coexistence mode

You can connect a WhatsApp number in **coexistence mode** — this keeps your existing WhatsApp Business app active on your phone alongside the API connection. Chat history and contacts from the app are imported, and messages stay in sync between the app and ChatFlows.

## Common problems

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Meta's pop-up window doesn't open">
    Your browser may be blocking pop-ups. Allow pop-ups for ChatFlows and click **Connect Channel** again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The number is already registered on WhatsApp">
    - **On the WhatsApp Business app?** That's fine. Connect it alongside the app (coexistence).
    - **On the personal WhatsApp app?** Delete that WhatsApp account on the phone first, or use another number.
    - **On another provider's WhatsApp API?** Ask that provider to release the number, then connect it here.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Templates don't send after connecting">
    Check that your WhatsApp Business account has a payment method in Meta's WhatsApp Manager, and that the template shows **Approved**.
    The reason for a failed message shows in **Setup → WhatsApp Message Log**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Messages stop at a daily limit">
    New numbers start with a limit on how many customers they can message in a day. Meta raises it as you send good-quality messages, and after business verification.
    This is a WhatsApp rule, not a ChatFlows one. Meta explains it under [messaging limits](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/messaging-limits).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

For other problems, see [Troubleshooting](/getting-started/troubleshooting).

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will I lose my WhatsApp Business app chats if I connect to ChatFlows?">
    No. If your number is on the WhatsApp Business app, connect it in coexistence mode and the app keeps working on your phone.
    ChatFlows imports your chat history and contacts, and messages stay in sync between the app and your Inbox.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I connect my personal WhatsApp number to ChatFlows?">
    Not while it is on the personal WhatsApp app. Delete that WhatsApp account on the phone first, or use another number.
    A number already on the WhatsApp Business app can connect as it is.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need Meta Business Verification to connect WhatsApp?">
    No. Business Verification is not required to connect, but Meta strongly recommends it.
    Without it your number stays on limited messaging tiers, which Meta explains under [messaging limits](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/messaging-limits).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I connect Instagram as well as WhatsApp?">
    No. ChatFlows works with WhatsApp and Messenger, and Instagram isn't available.
    To add a Facebook Page, see [Connecting Messenger](/core-setup/channels/messenger).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
