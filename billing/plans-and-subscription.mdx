This page explains how to manage a subscription you already have — including changing your plan, renewing, or cancelling it. To compare the plans and see what each one includes, visit the [Trial vs. paid plans](/getting-started/free-vs-paid).

<Info>
  Only the workspace **owner** can change, cancel, resume, or renew a subscription, or manage
  payment methods. Other team members can be given permission to **view** billing and invoices,
  but not to make changes.
</Info>

## Where to manage it

Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar to open the account menu:

- **My Subscription** — your plan, status, and renewal date, plus every action below.
- **Invoices** — your payment history.

<Note>
  On the free trial, there's nothing to bill, so you'll see **Available Plans** in this menu
  instead, ready when you want to upgrade.
</Note>

## Your subscription status

**My Subscription** shows a status badge so you always know where you stand:

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Free Plan** | You're on the 14-day free trial. It has an end date — see [Trial vs. paid plans](/getting-started/free-vs-paid). |
| **Active** | Your paid plan is current and working normally. |
| **Pending Payment** | A payment is waiting on review. See [Payments & invoices](/billing/payments-and-invoices). |
| **Canceled** | You've cancelled, but you keep access until the date shown. |
| **Suspended** | Your provider has paused your account. Contact them to resolve it. |
| **No Subscription** | Your trial or plan has ended and needs picking again before you can continue. |

<Note>
  The badge reads **Free Plan** while your trial is active — it doesn't show a countdown here.
  Keep an eye on your trial's end date so you're not caught out; see
  [Trial vs. paid plans](/getting-started/free-vs-paid).
</Note>

## Move from your trial to Professional

On **My Subscription**, use **Upgrade Plan** to move from your free trial to **Professional** —
whether you're doing it early or your trial has already ended. Pick your options and confirm.

Because the trial costs nothing, there's nothing to carry over from it — you simply pay for
Professional, and it's active as soon as payment goes through (or as soon as your provider approves
it, for a bank transfer).

<Tip>
  Everything you've already built carries over untouched — your contacts, chats, templates and
  flows are all still there on Professional.
</Tip>

## Upgrade or downgrade

On **My Subscription**, use **Upgrade Plan** or **Downgrade Plan** to move between the plans your
provider offers. A downgrade takes effect at your **next renewal** — you keep your current plan
until then. A scheduled downgrade shows as a banner on this page, with an **Undo** button if you
change your mind before it takes effect.

<Tip>
  Not sure which plan fits? [Trial vs. paid plans](/getting-started/free-vs-paid) shows what each
  one includes.
</Tip>

## Renewing

Your subscription renews on the date shown on **My Subscription**. There are two ways that happens.

- **Automatic renewal (default)** keeps your workspace running without you doing anything — it
  renews on the date and your saved payment method is charged.
- **Manual renewal** — if auto-renewal is off, a **Renew Now** button appears on **My Subscription**
  once you're close to your renewal date.

### Turning auto-renewal off

On **My Subscription**, next to **Auto-Renewal** (shown as **Enabled** or **Disabled**), use
**Turn Off**. You'll be asked to confirm — choose **Keep it on** to back out, or **Turn Off** to
continue. Your subscription won't renew on its own after that — use **Renew Now** yourself before
it lapses to keep your workspace active.

### If a renewal payment is coming up or fails

You may see a banner across your workspace as your renewal date approaches or if a payment doesn't
go through — it's there so you don't get caught out:

- **Before renewal** — a reminder that your subscription renews soon, with a **Renew Now** link.
- **Payment overdue** — a warning that your payment is late, with the date you need to renew by
  before your workspace is affected, and a **Pay Now** link.
- **Access reduced to billing only** — if it still isn't resolved, you'll only be able to reach
  billing until you pay — everything else is paused until then.

Paying at any point during this clears the banner and restores full access straight away.

## Cancel your subscription

On **My Subscription**, use **Cancel Subscription**. You'll be asked to confirm — choose
**Keep subscription** to back out, or **Cancel Subscription** to continue.

**You keep full access until the end of the period you've already paid for** — nothing is switched
off today, and you can change your mind and **Resume** any time before that date.

<Warning>
  Cancelling always takes effect at the **end of your current billing period** — there's no
  part-way cancellation or refund for unused days. After that date, if you haven't renewed or
  resumed, your workspace becomes **inactive** and you won't be able to send or receive. Renew or
  resume before then and everything is exactly as you left it.
</Warning>

## Resume a cancelled subscription

Changed your mind? While your subscription is still within its paid period, open **My Subscription**
and choose **Resume**. Your plan carries straight on, with everything intact — no need to set
anything up again.

## If your account is suspended

If your provider suspends your account, you'll see an **Account Suspended** screen instead of your
workspace, telling you to contact them for help. This isn't something you can undo yourself —
your provider needs to lift the suspension.
