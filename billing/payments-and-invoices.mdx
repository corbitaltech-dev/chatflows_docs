ChatFlows.io supports online and offline payment, coupon codes at checkout, and a full invoice
history — all from **My Subscription**.

## Paying online

Online payments cover subscribing to a plan, changing plans, and renewals. Checkout shows a live
total that updates instantly when you apply a coupon. **Your plan activates as soon as payment
succeeds** — you're taken straight back to your workspace.

<Tip>
  Paid but don't see it yet? Give it a moment and refresh the page — online payments usually
  reflect within a minute.
</Tip>

## Paying by bank transfer

Prefer to pay by bank transfer instead of online? You can. Your payment is safe throughout —
here's exactly what happens:

<Steps>
<Step title="Transfer to the bank details shown">
  You'll see the bank account (or UPI ID) to pay, along with any instructions shown there.
</Step>

<Step title="Submit your proof">
  On the **Submit Payment Proof** form, enter your **Bank Reference / UTR / UPI Transaction ID**.
  You can also attach a screenshot or receipt and add a note, though these are optional.
</Step>

<Step title="Your provider reviews and activates">
  Your request shows as **Pending Payment** while your provider checks it against the payment
  received. Once it's confirmed, your plan or renewal activates.
</Step>
</Steps>

Because a person checks each transfer, this isn't instant like an online payment — it activates
once the payment is confirmed. If anything's off, you'll see a **Payment Rejected** notice, so you
can fix it and submit again. Nothing is lost either way.

<Note>
  While a payment is under review, you can still use your workspace normally — only the
  **Upgrade Plan** and **Cancel Subscription** actions are disabled until the decision comes back.
  The one exception is a brand-new signup awaiting its first payment: that account stays on an
  activation screen until it's approved.
</Note>

<Warning>
  You can only have one payment under review at a time. If you submit again while a request is
  still pending, you'll be asked to wait for the first one to be reviewed.
</Warning>

## Coupons

At checkout — whether subscribing, upgrading, or renewing — you can apply a coupon code. The
total recalculates live so you can see the discount before you confirm payment.

<Note>
  Coupons apply to your **current purchase only**. They don't carry over to your next renewal, so
  a future renewal will be charged at full price.
</Note>

<Accordion title="My coupon code didn't work" icon="info">
  A code is usually turned down because it's **expired**, has **already been used** on your
  account, or **doesn't match** the plan you're buying. The checkout tells you which — read the
  message, adjust if you can, and try again.
</Accordion>

## Invoices

Every billed transaction generates an invoice under **Invoices**:

- Browse a table of past invoices.
- **View** an invoice's full breakdown.
- **Download** it as a PDF for your records.

<Note>
  Team members need permission to see invoices — the same permission covers both viewing and
  downloading, so anyone who can open an invoice can also save the PDF.
</Note>

<Warning>
  Only the workspace **owner** can pay or apply coupons.
</Warning>
