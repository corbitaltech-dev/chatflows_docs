You pay for two different things:

- **Your ChatFlows plan.** Billed by Shopify. See [Plan and billing](/billing/plan-and-billing).
- **WhatsApp messages.** Billed by **Meta**, directly to you. ChatFlows adds nothing on top.

## What Meta charges for

Meta charges for **template messages** that are delivered. The price depends on two things:

- **The template category:** Marketing, Utility or Authentication.
- **The customer's country.**

| Category | Examples | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Utility** | Order placed, shipped, delivered, COD payment received | Lower |
| **Marketing** | Cart reminders, offers, new products, campaigns | Higher |
| **Authentication** | One-time passcodes | Lower |

## What's free

- **Replies within 24 hours.** When a customer writes to you, you can reply freely for 24 hours. These replies are not charged.
- **Utility templates inside the 24 hours.** A Utility template sent while the 24 hours are open is not charged.
- **Messages that are not delivered** are not charged.

## Estimate your monthly bill

Count how many messages of each kind you send, and multiply by Meta's rate for your customers' country.

Here is an example for a store that gets 300 orders and 200 abandoned carts a month.
**The rates are made up to show the math. Check Meta's real rates for your country.**

| Messages | How many | Example rate | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Order placed + shipped + delivered (Utility) | 300 × 3 = 900 | 0.10 | 90 |
| Cart reminders (Marketing) | 200 | 0.80 | 160 |
| One campaign to 1,000 customers (Marketing) | 1,000 | 0.80 | 800 |
| Replies to customers within 24 hours | — | Free | 0 |
| **Total** | | | **1,050** |

<Tip>
  Campaigns cost the most. Send them to a group that wants them, not to everyone.
</Tip>

## Where Meta bills you

Meta bills your WhatsApp Business account. You add a payment method in Meta's own WhatsApp Manager.
Without a payment method, Meta may stop your template messages from sending.

<Note>
  Meta sets these prices and changes them from time to time. This is a WhatsApp rule, not a ChatFlows one.
  See Meta's current rates on its [pricing page](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing).
</Note>
