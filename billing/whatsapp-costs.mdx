You pay for two different things:

- **Your ChatFlows plan.** Billed by Shopify. See [Plan and billing](/billing/plan-and-billing).
- **WhatsApp messages.** Billed by **Meta**, directly to you. ChatFlows adds nothing on top.

<Info>
  **Meta changed its prices on October 1, 2026.** Replies and Utility templates inside the 24-hour window used to be free. Now your first **1,000 replies** each month are free, and Utility templates are charged wherever you send them. This page shows the new rules.
</Info>

## What Meta charges for

Meta charges for messages **your business sends** that are **delivered**. The price depends on two things:

- **The kind of message.** Meta calls this the category.
- **The customer's country.**

| Category | Examples | What Meta charges |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Utility** | Order placed, shipped, delivered, COD payment received | A lower rate, every time |
| **Marketing** | Cart reminders, offers, new products, campaigns | A higher rate, every time |
| **Authentication** | One-time passcodes | A lower rate, every time |
| **Service** | Your replies, in your own words, after a customer messages you | Free for the first 1,000 each month, then charged |

Sending lots of Utility messages? Meta lowers the Utility rate as your monthly volume grows.

## What's free

- **Messages customers send you.** Incoming messages are never charged.
- **Your first 1,000 replies each month.** Each WhatsApp number gets 1,000 free replies a month. Unused ones don't carry over to the next month.
- **Messages that aren't delivered.** You only pay for messages that reach the phone.
- **Chats that start from a click-to-WhatsApp ad.** When a customer messages you from your ad on Facebook and you reply within 24 hours, your messages to them are free for up to 7 days.

## Estimate your monthly bill

Count how many messages of each kind you send, and multiply by Meta's rate for your customers' country.

Here's an example for a store that gets 300 orders and 200 abandoned carts a month, sends one campaign, and replies to customers 600 times.

<Warning>
  **These rates are made up to show the math. They are not Meta's prices.** Check Meta's real rates for your customers' country before you plan.
</Warning>

| Messages | How many | Example rate | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Order placed + shipped + delivered (Utility) | 300 × 3 = 900 | $0.01 | $9 |
| Cart reminders (Marketing) | 200 | $0.05 | $10 |
| One campaign to 1,000 customers (Marketing) | 1,000 | $0.05 | $50 |
| Replies to customers (Service) | 600 | Free (under 1,000) | $0 |
| **Total** | | | **$69** |

In this example, the campaign is most of the bill.

## Keep your bill down

- **Send campaigns to a group that wants them**, not to everyone. They cost the most.
- **Keep order updates as Utility.** A sales line can turn them into Marketing, which costs more. See [Template examples](/messaging/template-examples).
- **Two cart reminders are usually enough.** Each one is a Marketing message.

## Where Meta bills you

Meta bills your WhatsApp Business account. You add a payment method in Meta's own WhatsApp Manager.

Without a payment method, your template messages may not send, and your replies stop after the free 1,000 each month.

<Note>
  Meta sets these prices and can change them at the start of each quarter. This is a WhatsApp rule, not a ChatFlows one.
  See Meta's current rules and rates on its [pricing page](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing).
</Note>
