Connect your Shopify store to automatically send WhatsApp messages when customers **place an order, their order ships, their order is delivered, or they leave without completing a purchase**.

Find it under **Settings → Shopify**.

<Note>
Shopify is included in every ChatFlows.io plan. New to ChatFlows.io? Start with the [Shopify quick start](/getting-started/shopify-quick-start).
</Note>

## Connecting your store

You connect your store by installing ChatFlows.io from the Shopify App Store.

<Steps>
<Step title="Install from the Shopify App Store">
  Open [ChatFlows.io on the Shopify App Store](https://apps.shopify.com/chatflows) and click **Install**.
</Step>

<Step title="Approve the permissions">
  Shopify shows you what ChatFlows.io needs access to — your **customer data**, your **staff and contributor data**, and your **store data**, which covers customers, products, orders and your online store. Click **Install** to approve.

  <Figure
    src="/settings/img/shopify-install.png"
    alt="Shopify's install screen for ChatFlows.io, listing View customer data, View staff and contributor data, and View store data, with Cancel and Install buttons"
    caption="Image shows the Shopify permissions screen."
  />
</Step>

<Step title="Choose a plan, or sign in">
  Pick a plan and approve it on Shopify's billing screen. Your workspace is created and linked to the store. Already have a ChatFlows.io workspace? Choose **Sign in to link this store** instead.
</Step>

<Step title="Your store shows as connected">
  In ChatFlows.io, **Settings → Shopify** shows the store as **Connected**.

  <Figure
    src="/settings/img/shopify-connected.png"
    alt="The Shopify Store card showing a connected store, a Disconnect button and the Sync contacts section"
    caption="Image shows a connected Shopify store."
  />
</Step>
</Steps>


## What you can build

After you connect your store, Shopify events such as a **new order, shipping updates, order cancellation, or an abandoned cart** can automatically start an automation flow.

Choose the event when creating your flow. See [Shopify triggers](/automation/flow-builder#shopify) for the full list and when each one runs.

## Your first message must be a template

Every Shopify flow starts when something happens in your store, not when a customer messages you. For events like **abandoned carts, new orders, and shipping updates,** the customer usually hasn't contacted you yet, so you can't send a regular WhatsApp message.

Start every Shopify flow with a [**Template Message**](/automation/flow-steps/basic#whatsapp-template). Once the customer replies, you can continue the conversation with normal text messages within [**the 24-hour messaging window**](/getting-started/concepts#two-whatsapp-rules-that-shape-everything).

## How abandoned carts work

An **abandoned cart** is a customer who reached your checkout and left without paying.

Each cart-abandoned automation waits for its own **Send after (minutes)** delay: one hour unless you change it, and anything from 5 minutes to 24 hours after checkout starts. When the time is up, ChatFlows.io checks the checkout again. If the shopper has ordered, nothing is sent.

Want a second reminder? Create a second cart-abandoned automation with a longer delay, for example 60 minutes for a friendly nudge and 1,380 minutes for a last reminder the next day. Each one checks for an order before it sends.

<Warning>
Don't add a **Wait** step inside one cart automation to send a second reminder. A wait does not check the checkout again, so a shopper who has already ordered would still get it.
</Warning>

Add `order.recovery_url` to your message to send them straight back to their checkout, with everything still in the basket.

## Order details in your messages

Your messages can include the customer's own order details, what they bought, the total, tracking link and more. Type `@` while writing and pick what you need.

See [Merge fields](/messaging/merge-fields#available-fields) for everything you can use.

## Bringing in your existing customers

Click **Sync contacts** to import the customers already in your store into [Contacts](/contacts/managing-contacts).

Only customers **with a phone number** are imported. The rest are skipped.

## Disconnecting

Click **Disconnect** and confirm. Uninstalling ChatFlows.io from your Shopify admin also disconnects the store and cancels your plan. Shopify events stop triggering your automation flows immediately. Your contacts stay in your workspace. Automation flows are not deleted. If you reconnect later, your existing flows will work again.
