Go to **Settings → General** to set how dates, times and phone numbers appear across your workspace.

<Figure
  src="/settings/img/general-form.png"
  alt="The Organization Information form with fields for organization name, subdomain, timezone, date format and time format"
  caption="Image shows the General settings page."
/>

- **Timezone** — the timezone your workspace uses.
- **Date Format** — choose from `2026-02-13`, `13/02/2026`, `02/13/2026`, or `February 13, 2026`.
- **Time Format** — **24 Hours** or **12 Hours**.
- **Default Country** — pre-selects the country on every phone number field.

## Where it's used

Your **timezone** is used throughout the workspace. Scheduled campaigns and business hours both use this timezone.

The date and time formats control how dates and times are displayed across the workspace, including customers, campaigns, chats, and reports.
