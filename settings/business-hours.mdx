Go to **Settings → Business Hours** to set when you are open. Your [AI Assistant](#how-your-ai-assistant-uses-these-hours) uses these hours to know when your team is around to reply.

<Note>
Business hours only affect the AI Assistant. They do not change when campaigns send or when your team can reply in the inbox.
</Note>

## Weekly hours

Each day has an On/Off switch. Turn it on to set your opening and closing times. Turn it off to mark the day as Closed.

New workspaces are open Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:00, with Saturday and Sunday closed by default.

All business hours use your workspace timezone, which you can change in [General](/settings/general).

## Closed dates

Use **Closed dates (holidays)** to block specific dates, such as public holidays or special closures. On these dates you count as closed all day.

Select a date and click **Add**. To remove a date, click the × next to it.

## How your AI Assistant uses these hours

Open your AI Assistant and go to the **Behaviour** tab. Turn on **Respect business hours**. The switch says it "Uses your workspace business hours, set in Settings."

Then choose what happens under **Outside business hours**:

- **Reply as usual** — the assistant still answers. It knows you are closed, so it won't promise a quick reply from a person.
- **Hand off to a human** — the assistant steps back and leaves the chat for your team.
- **Stay silent** — the assistant pauses and does not reply.

You count as closed when a message comes in outside that day's hours, on a day switched off, or on a closed date. When the assistant hands off or pauses, a private note in the chat tells your team why.
