Go to **Settings → Billing Details** to set the company information that appears in the **Bill To** section of your invoices.

<Figure
  src="/settings/img/billing-details-form.png"
  alt="The Billing Details form with fields for company name, email, GST number, country, state, ZIP code and address"
  caption="Image shows the Billing Details page."
/>

- **Company Name** — The name shown on your invoices.
- **Company Email** — The email address shown on your invoices.
- **GST Number** — Your tax number (optional).
- **Country** — Select your country.
- **State / Province** – Your state or province.
- **ZIP / Postal Code** – Your ZIP or postal code.
- **Company Address** – Your business address.

<Note>
These billing details appear on all upcoming invoices only. Invoices already generated are not updated.
</Note>

## Do these details change what I pay?

No. These details only change what is printed on your invoices. Any tax is shown as its own line on the invoice.
