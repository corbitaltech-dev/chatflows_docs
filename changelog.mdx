<Update label="18 November 2026" description="Confirm cash-on-delivery orders on WhatsApp" tags={["COD", "Shopify", "Automations"]} rss={{ date: "2026-11-18" }}>

## Cash-on-delivery confirmation

When a customer places a cash-on-delivery order, ChatFlows asks them on WhatsApp to confirm or cancel it, and updates the order in Shopify for you:

- **Confirm order** tags it **COD-CONFIRMED**, so your team knows it's safe to pack.
- **Cancel order** cancels it in Shopify, so you don't ship a parcel nobody will pay for.
- Switch on **Hold new COD orders until the buyer confirms** and nobody can fulfill an order before the customer confirms it.
- If the customer doesn't answer, send a reminder, or cancel the order after the time you choose.
- A separate message thanks the customer when their cash-on-delivery payment arrives.

[COD orders →](/shopify/cod-orders)

</Update>
