Every message that starts a conversation must be an approved **template**.
Use these examples as a starting point. Change the wording to fit your store.

In the examples, **numbers in double curly brackets** are variables, such as the customer's name or order number.
When you use the template in an automation, you fill each variable with a [merge field](/messaging/merge-fields), like the order number.

To add one, go to **Message Templates → WhatsApp → Add Template**.

## Tips for getting approved

- **Pick the right category.** Order updates are **Utility**. Cart reminders, offers and new products are **Marketing**.
- **Keep Utility templates about the order.** A discount or "shop now" line in a Utility template can be rejected or moved to Marketing.
- **Don't start or end with a variable.** Put some words before the first variable and after the last one.
- **Fill in example values.** Meta uses them to check the template. Use realistic examples, like an order number.
- **Keep it short and clear.** Say what happened and what the customer can do next.

<Note>
  Meta decides the final category, and it can change it. This is a WhatsApp rule, not a ChatFlows.io one.
  Meta explains categories and reviews in its [template overview](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/templates/overview).
</Note>

## Order updates (Utility)

**Order placed**

```text
Hi {{1}}, thanks for your order {{2}}! We're getting it ready and will message you when it ships.
```

**Order shipped**

```text
Good news, {{1}}! Your order {{2}} is on its way. Track it here: {{3}}
```

**Out for delivery**

```text
Hi {{1}}, your order {{2}} is out for delivery today. Please keep your phone close.
```

**Delivered**

```text
Hi {{1}}, your order {{2}} has been delivered. We hope you love it! Reply here if anything isn't right.
```

**Order cancelled**

```text
Hi {{1}}, your order {{2}} has been cancelled. If you didn't ask for this, reply here and we'll help.
```

## COD (Utility)

**COD payment received**

```text
Hi {{1}}, we've received your cash payment for order {{2}}. Thank you for shopping with us!
```

Use it with the **COD order confirmed** event. See [COD orders](/shopify/cod-orders).

## Cart reminders (Marketing)

**First reminder**

```text
Hi {{1}}, you left something in your cart. It's still waiting for you: {{2}}
```

**Last reminder**

```text
Hi {{1}}, your cart is about to expire. Complete your order here: {{2}}
```

Fill the link variable with the **Cart Recovery Link** merge field. See [Recover abandoned carts](/shopify/cart-recovery).

## Feedback (Marketing)

**After delivery**

```text
Hi {{1}}, how was your order {{2}}? Reply with a number from 1 to 5. Your feedback helps us get better.
```

Send it from an **Order delivered** automation with a **Delay** step, for example 2 days.

## Campaigns (Marketing)

**New collection**

```text
Hi {{1}}, our new collection has just arrived. Take a look before it sells out: {{2}}
```

**Back in stock**

```text
Hi {{1}}, good news: {{2}} is back in stock. Order yours here: {{3}}
```
