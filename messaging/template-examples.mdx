Every message that starts a conversation must be an approved **template**.
Use these examples as a starting point. Change the wording to fit your store.

In the examples, **numbers in double curly brackets** are variables, such as the customer's name or order number. Each one is filled in for every customer when the message is sent.

## Utility or Marketing?

Every template has a **category**. It decides what the message can say and what Meta charges for it.

| Category | Use it for | What Meta charges |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Utility** | Updates about an order the customer already placed | Free while the customer's 24-hour window is open, charged outside it |
| **Marketing** | Cart reminders, offers, new products, feedback requests | Charged every time |

So keep order updates as **Utility**, and don't mix a sales line into them. See [WhatsApp costs](/billing/whatsapp-costs) for the details.

## Tips for getting approved

- **Pick the right category.** Use the table above.
- **Keep Utility templates about the order.** A discount or "shop now" line in a Utility template can be rejected or moved to Marketing.
- **Don't start or end with a variable.** Put some words before the first variable and after the last one. Every example below follows this.
- **Fill in example values.** Meta uses them to check the template. Use realistic examples, like an order number.
- **Keep it short and clear.** Say what happened and what the customer can do next.

<Note>
  Meta decides the final category, and it can change it. This is a WhatsApp rule, not a ChatFlows one.
  Meta explains categories and reviews in its [template overview](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/templates/overview).
</Note>

## How to use an example

1. Go to **Message Templates → WhatsApp → Add Template**.
2. Pick the category shown for the example.
3. Copy the text into the message body and change the wording to fit your store.
4. Submit it to Meta. When it shows **Approved**, use it in an automation.
5. In the automation, fill each variable with the [merge field](/messaging/merge-fields) listed under the example.

## Utility templates

Use these with the Shopify order events. See [Send order updates](/shopify/order-updates).

### Order placed

```text
Hi {{1}}, thanks for your order {{2}}! We're getting it ready and will message you when it ships.
```

Fill with: `{{1}}` **First Name**, `{{2}}` **Order Number**.

### Order partially shipped

```text
Hi {{1}}, part of your order {{2}} is on its way. You can track it at {{3}}. We'll message you when the rest ships.
```

Fill with: `{{1}}` **First Name**, `{{2}}` **Order Number**, `{{3}}` **Tracking URL**.

### Order shipped

```text
Good news, {{1}}! Your order {{2}} is on its way. You can track it at {{3}} anytime.
```

Fill with: `{{1}}` **First Name**, `{{2}}` **Order Number**, `{{3}}` **Tracking URL**.

### Out for delivery

```text
Hi {{1}}, your order {{2}} is out for delivery today. Please keep your phone close.
```

Fill with: `{{1}}` **First Name**, `{{2}}` **Order Number**.

### Delivered

```text
Hi {{1}}, your order {{2}} has been delivered. We hope you love it! Reply here if anything isn't right.
```

Fill with: `{{1}}` **First Name**, `{{2}}` **Order Number**.

### Order cancelled

```text
Hi {{1}}, your order {{2}} has been cancelled. If you didn't ask for this, reply here and we'll help.
```

Fill with: `{{1}}` **First Name**, `{{2}}` **Order Number**.

### COD payment received

```text
Hi {{1}}, we've received your cash payment for order {{2}}. Thank you for shopping with us!
```

Fill with: `{{1}}` **First Name**, `{{2}}` **Order Number**. Use it with the **COD payment received** event. See [COD orders](/shopify/cod-orders).

## Marketing templates

These are charged every time they're sent, so send them when they're most likely to help.

### Cart reminder: first

```text
Hi {{1}}, you left something in your cart. It's still waiting for you at {{2}} whenever you're ready.
```

Fill with: `{{1}}` **First Name**, `{{2}}` **Cart Recovery Link**. See [Recover abandoned carts](/shopify/cart-recovery).

### Cart reminder: last

```text
Hi {{1}}, just a reminder that your cart is still saved. You can finish your order at {{2}} before items sell out.
```

Fill with: `{{1}}` **First Name**, `{{2}}` **Cart Recovery Link**.

<Tip>
  Rather than putting the link in the text, you can add a button, such as **Complete my order**, and use the **Cart Recovery Link** as its link.
</Tip>

### Feedback after delivery

```text
Hi {{1}}, how was your order {{2}}? Reply with a number from 1 to 5. Your feedback helps us get better.
```

Fill with: `{{1}}` **First Name**, `{{2}}` **Order Number**. Send it from an **Order delivered** automation with a **Delay** step, for example 2 days.

### Campaign: new collection

```text
Hi {{1}}, our new collection has just arrived. Take a look on our store before it sells out!
```

Fill with: `{{1}}` **First Name**. Add a button that links to your collection page.

### Campaign: back in stock

```text
Hi {{1}}, good news: {{2}} is back in stock. Tap below to order yours.
```

Fill with: `{{1}}` **First Name**, `{{2}}` the product name. Add a button that links to the product page.
