Your Shopify store lets ChatFlows.io send WhatsApp messages automatically when customers **place an order, their order ships, their order is delivered, or they leave without completing a purchase**.

Your store is connected for you when you [install ChatFlows.io from Shopify](/getting-started/install-from-shopify). There's nothing to set up here first.

To check the connection, go to **Settings → Shopify** in your workspace.

## What the status means

The store card shows one of these:

- **Connected** — everything works. Store events can start your automations.
- **Connecting…** — setup is still finishing. It changes to **Connected** a moment later.
- **Needs attention** — something stopped working. The card shows the reason Shopify gave. See [Fix a connection that needs attention](#fix-a-connection-that-needs-attention).

If your store isn't connected, you see the **Connect Shopify** form instead of the card.

## Connect with your store domain

You only need this if your store isn't connected — for example, after you disconnected it.

<Steps>
<Step title="Enter your store domain">
  On **Settings → Shopify**, enter your Shopify store domain exactly as Shopify provides it, ending with `.myshopify.com`. For example, `your-store.myshopify.com`.

  <Figure
    src="/settings/img/connect-shopify.png"
    alt="The Connect Shopify card with a Store domain field and a Connect Shopify button"
    caption="Image shows the Connect Shopify form."
  />
</Step>

<Step title="Click Connect Shopify">
  You are taken to Shopify. If you're reconnecting the same store, the button says **Reconnect Shopify**.
</Step>

<Step title="Approve the permissions">
  Shopify shows you what ChatFlows.io needs access to — your **customer data**, your **staff and contributor data**, and your **store data**, which covers customers, products, orders and your online store. Click **Install** to approve.
</Step>

<Step title="You come back automatically">
  The status shows **Connecting…** and then **Connected** a moment later.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Shoppers can only get WhatsApp messages if your Shopify checkout asks for a phone number.
</Note>

## Fix a connection that needs attention

When the card says **Needs attention**, read the reason it shows. Then use one of these buttons.

- **Reconnect** — takes you back to Shopify to approve ChatFlows.io again. Use it when Shopify's access was removed, or when ChatFlows.io needs a permission your store hasn't given yet.
- **Retry setup** — runs the store setup again, without going to Shopify. Use it when your store's access still works but setup didn't finish, for example after a problem has been fixed. You only see it when a retry can help.

<Tip>
  Seeing **Delivery updates are off**? Your **Out for delivery** and **Delivered** automations need one more permission. Click **Reconnect** and approve it in Shopify. If there's no **Reconnect** button in that message, contact support.
</Tip>

## What you can build

Once your store is connected, Shopify events such as a **new order, shipping updates, order cancellation, or an abandoned cart** can start an automation.

Choose the event when you create your automation. See [Shopify triggers](/automation/flow-builder#shopify) for the full list and when each one runs.

## Your first message must be a template

Every Shopify automation starts when something happens in your store, not when a customer messages you. For events like **abandoned carts, new orders, and shipping updates,** the customer usually hasn't contacted you yet, so you can't send a regular WhatsApp message.

Start every Shopify automation with a [**Template Message**](/automation/flow-steps/basic#whatsapp-template). Once the customer replies, you can continue the conversation with normal text messages within [**the 24-hour messaging window**](/getting-started/concepts#two-whatsapp-rules-that-shape-everything).

## How abandoned carts work

An **abandoned cart** is a customer who reached your checkout and left without paying.

Each cart-recovery automation has its own **Send after (minutes)** setting. You choose how long to wait — from 5 minutes up to 1,440 minutes (one day). If you leave it empty, it waits 60 minutes.

If the customer pays before the wait is over, no message is sent. You can run two automations with different waits — a quick nudge and a later follow-up.

Add `order.recovery_url` to your message to send them straight back to their checkout, with everything still in the basket.

See [Recover abandoned carts](/shopify/cart-recovery) for a step-by-step guide.

## Order details in your messages

Your messages can include the customer's own order details, what they bought, the total, tracking link and more. Type `@` while writing and pick what you need.

See [Merge fields](/messaging/merge-fields#available-fields) for everything you can use.

## Bringing in your existing customers

Click **Sync contacts** to import the customers already in your store into [Customers](/contacts/managing-contacts).

Only customers **with a phone number** are imported. The rest are skipped. If a customer has tags in Shopify, they come across as customer tags.

## Disconnecting

Click **Disconnect** and confirm. Shopify events stop starting your automations right away.

Your customers stay in your workspace. Your automations are not deleted. If you connect the store again later, your existing automations work again.
