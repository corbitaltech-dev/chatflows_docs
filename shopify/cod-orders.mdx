A cash-on-delivery (COD) order that the buyer never meant to accept costs you shipping both ways.
ChatFlows asks every COD buyer to confirm their order on WhatsApp, then tags the order in Shopify, so your team packs only the confirmed ones.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="A COD order is placed" icon="banknote">
    The **COD order placed** event starts your automation, and ChatFlows tags the order **COD-PENDING** in Shopify. If you turned on **Hold new COD orders until the buyer confirms**, the order is also put **On hold**, so nobody can fulfill it yet.
  </Step>

  <Step title="The buyer gets a WhatsApp message" icon="message-circle">
    The message shows what they ordered and the amount, with two buttons: **Confirm order** and **Cancel order**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="The buyer taps a button" icon="mouse-pointer-click">
    - **Confirm order**: the tag changes to **COD-CONFIRMED**, the hold is released, and the buyer gets a thank-you.
    - **Cancel order**: the tag changes to **COD-CANCELLED**, a note is added, the order is **cancelled in Shopify** (items go back in stock, nothing is refunded), and the buyer is told it won't ship.
    - **They type a reply instead**: they're asked to tap one of the buttons.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Your team packs the confirmed orders" icon="package">
    In Shopify, filter your orders by the **COD-CONFIRMED** tag. With the hold switched on, unconfirmed orders can't be fulfilled at all.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If one buyer has two COD orders waiting, each tap updates the order whose message they tapped. Their first answer counts: tapping **Cancel order** after **Confirm order** on the same message changes nothing.

## Before you start

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Permission to change orders" icon="tags">
    Tagging, holding and cancelling need ChatFlows' permission to update orders in your store. Stores connected before ChatFlows asked for it see **Order changes are off** in **Settings → Shopify**. Click **Reconnect** and approve it in Shopify.

    ChatFlows only adds and removes tags, adds notes, holds, releases and cancels orders, and only when your automation does it. It never changes products or prices.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="An approved message template" icon="file-text">
    The first message to a buyer must be a template that Meta has approved. This is a WhatsApp rule, not a ChatFlows one. Meta explains it in its [template overview](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/templates/overview).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Phone numbers at checkout" icon="smartphone">
    ChatFlows finds the buyer by the phone number on the order. Make sure your Shopify checkout asks for one.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the template" icon="file-text">
    Go to **Message Templates → WhatsApp**, open the **Template Collection**, and click **Use Template** on **Shopify: COD Order Verification**. Keep the name **shopify_cod_confirmation**, adjust the wording if you like, and submit it to Meta.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add the automation" icon="zap">
    Go to **Automations**, click **Flow Collection**, choose **Shopify Store**, and clone **Shopify: COD Order Verification**. Pick your WhatsApp number.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Turn it on" icon="toggle-right">
    Once Meta approves the template, turn the automation on. Every new COD order now gets the message.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Optional: hold new COD orders" icon="pause-circle">
    In **Settings → Shopify**, turn on **Hold new COD orders until the buyer confirms**. New COD orders are then put **On hold** in Shopify until the buyer taps **Confirm order**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Hold new COD orders until the buyer confirms

With this switch on, every new COD order is put **On hold** in Shopify the moment it arrives, so it can't be fulfilled by mistake. **Confirm order** releases it; **Cancel order** cancels it.

- A COD order is only held when an automation on **COD order placed** is turned on and the order has a phone number. That way every held order gets the WhatsApp question.
- ChatFlows only ever releases its own hold. A hold from another app or your team stays.
- Turning the switch off doesn't release orders that are already on hold.

## When the buyer doesn't reply

In the automation, open the **Wait for Reply** step and set **If there is no reply within**, for example 24 hours. A second, red exit appears on the step. Connect it to what should happen, such as a reminder template, or a **Cancel Shopify Order** step to cancel unconfirmed orders. See [Wait for Reply](/automation/flow-steps/advanced#wait-for-reply).

Without a time limit, an unanswered order keeps the **COD-PENDING** tag, and stays on hold if the switch is on, until you deal with it in Shopify.

<Tip>
  Want different tags? Open the automation and change the **Update Shopify Order** steps. See [Update Shopify Order](/automation/flow-steps/integrations#update-shopify-order).
</Tip>

<Warning>
  Tell your team to pack only orders tagged **COD-CONFIRMED**, rather than skipping orders tagged **COD-PENDING**. An order with no phone number never gets a message or a tag.
</Warning>

## How ChatFlows spots a COD order

An order counts as COD when its payment method in Shopify says **cash on delivery**, or has **COD** as a separate word, like "Cash on Delivery (COD)".
In your messages, the **Payment Method** merge field then shows **Cash on Delivery**.

## The COD events

| Event | When it runs |
| --- | --- |
| **COD order placed** | A COD order is placed, before the buyer has paid. Use it to ask them to confirm. |
| **COD payment received** | The COD order is marked as **paid** in Shopify, after the buyer pays the courier. Use it to thank them. |

A COD order also gets every other order event, such as **Order placed**, **Order shipped** and **Order delivered**. See [Send order updates](/shopify/order-updates).

<Note>
  **Order placed** runs for COD orders too. If you also send an order confirmation on **Order placed**, a COD buyer gets both messages. Keep both, or word the order confirmation so it reads well next to the COD question.
</Note>

## What ChatFlows doesn't do

- It never cancels an order that is already paid or shipped, even if the buyer taps **Cancel order**. You handle those in Shopify.
- It doesn't refund anything when it cancels. A COD order hasn't taken any money.

## Already using the old COD automation?

The earlier ready-made COD flow started on **COD payment received** and didn't tag, release or cancel orders. Clone **Shopify: COD Order Verification** again from the Flow Collection, then turn the old one off.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can ChatFlows verify COD orders before they ship?">
    Yes. ChatFlows asks each COD buyer to confirm or cancel on WhatsApp and tags the order **COD-CONFIRMED** or **COD-CANCELLED** in Shopify, so you ship only confirmed orders.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does ChatFlows cancel COD orders in Shopify for me?">
    Yes, when the buyer taps **Cancel order**: the ready-made automation tags the order **COD-CANCELLED** and cancels it in Shopify, with no refund. It never cancels an order that is already paid or shipped.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can ChatFlows stop my team from shipping unconfirmed COD orders?">
    Yes. Turn on **Hold new COD orders until the buyer confirms** in **Settings → Shopify**. Each new COD order is put on hold in Shopify and released only when the buyer confirms.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What else can ChatFlows change in my Shopify store?">
    Only order tags, notes, holds and cancellations, and only when one of your automations does it. ChatFlows never changes products or prices.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will ChatFlows recognize my cash on delivery payment method?">
    Yes, if its name in Shopify says **cash on delivery** or has **COD** as a separate word. A payment method without either isn't treated as COD.
    See [How ChatFlows spots a COD order](#how-chatflows-spots-a-cod-order).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will a COD buyer without a phone number get a WhatsApp message?">
    No. ChatFlows finds the customer by the phone number on the order, and orders without one don't get a message or a tag.
    See [Send order updates](/shopify/order-updates#how-chatflows-finds-the-customer).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to change my Shopify theme to verify COD orders?">
    No. ChatFlows doesn't add anything to your storefront or theme.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
