Many stores sell with cash on delivery (COD). COD buyers get the same WhatsApp order and shipping updates as every other buyer, and you can send a message when their COD payment is received. This page explains how it works.

## How ChatFlows spots a COD order

An order counts as COD when its payment method in Shopify has **cash on delivery** or **COD** in its name.
In your messages, the **Payment Method** merge field then shows **Cash on Delivery**.

## What COD buyers get

A COD order gets the same updates as any other order. Use the Shopify events in your automations:

| Event | When it runs for a COD order |
| --- | --- |
| **Order placed** | The order is placed. |
| **Order shipped** and **Order partially shipped** | The order, or part of it, ships. |
| **Order out for delivery** and **Order delivered** | The courier is delivering it, or it arrived. Only if your carrier sends tracking to Shopify. |
| **COD payment received** | The COD order is marked as **paid** in Shopify, after the buyer pays the courier. |
| **Order cancelled** | The order is cancelled. |

See [Send order updates](/shopify/order-updates) to set these up.

<Note>
  **COD payment received** runs after payment, not before the order ships.
  Use it to thank the buyer once they have paid.
</Note>

## What ChatFlows doesn't do yet

ChatFlows doesn't ask buyers to confirm or cancel a COD order before it ships.
It also doesn't change or cancel orders in Shopify. ChatFlows only reads your store.

An automation can't send COD orders down a different path from prepaid orders.
If you want a different message for COD buyers, use the **COD payment received** event, or add the **Payment Method** merge field to your order message.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can ChatFlows cancel or edit a COD order in Shopify for me?">
    No. ChatFlows only reads your store and never changes or cancels orders. You manage COD orders in Shopify as usual.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will ChatFlows recognize my cash on delivery payment method?">
    Yes, if its name in Shopify contains **cash on delivery** or **COD**. A payment method without either wording isn't treated as COD.
    See [How ChatFlows spots a COD order](#how-chatflows-spots-a-cod-order).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will a COD buyer without a phone number get a WhatsApp message?">
    No. ChatFlows finds the customer by the phone number on the order, and orders without one don't get a message.
    See [Send order updates](/shopify/order-updates#how-chatflows-finds-the-customer).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to change my Shopify theme to handle COD orders on WhatsApp?">
    No. ChatFlows doesn't add anything to your storefront or theme. It reads your orders from Shopify.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
